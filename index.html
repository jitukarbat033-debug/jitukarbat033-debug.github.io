Privacy Policy

Lotus Music Player is built as an offline media utility. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Device Storage Access:

The app requires permission to access your device's audio files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) strictly to discover, load, and play your local music files. We do NOT collect, upload, or share your personal files or music library.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob):

The app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements when an active internet connection is available. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Contact Us:

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy or the app, please feel free to contact developer support.
